# Project Neigo | Menghidupkan cerita AI dengan ingatan dan imajinasi.

**Project Neigo** is a state-of-the-art, proprietary AI orchestration platform designed to redefine the boundaries of interactive storytelling and digital companionship. By synthesizing advanced agentic workflows, non-linear memory retrieval, and rich media integration, Project Neigo delivers a high-fidelity narrative experience that functions as a living, breathing digital universe.

---

## 💎 The Vision: Solving the "Static AI" Limitation

The current landscape of AI entertainment is limited by "Linear Staticity"—where interactions are transactional, short-lived, and lack genuine evolution. **Project Neigo** solves this by treating AI characters not as mere chatbots, but as **Dynamic Narrative Entities** capable of long-term memory, emotional growth, and complex environmental awareness.

Our ecosystem is built for a future where digital entertainment is personalized, immersive, and infinitely scalable, bridging the gap between static Visual Novels and unpredictable AI interactions.

---

## 🧠 Proprietary AI Architecture: Multi-Pass Agentic Orchestration

The core of Project Neigo’s competitive advantage lies in its **Proprietary Multi-Pass Orchestration Engine**. Unlike standard applications that rely on a single, context-heavy prompt, Project Neigo breaks every interaction into a distributed, long-chain reasoning pipeline.

### 1. The Director (Strategic Intelligence & RAG)
The Director functions as the "brain" of the ecosystem. It manages the **Contextual Audit** by:
*   **Vector-Memory Retrieval**: Utilizing `pgvector` to pull non-linear memories from thousands of previous turns.
*   **World-State Synchronization**: Cross-referencing current actions against the "Global Lorebook" to prevent contradictions.
*   **Narrative Goal Setting**: Defining the objective of the current turn before any character logic is executed.

### 2. The Reactor (Cognitive & Emotional Emulation)
This layer handles the "hidden" logic of personality.
*   **Subconscious Monologue**: Before generating dialogue, the system simulates the character's internal thoughts, emotional trajectory, and subconscious bias.
*   **Relationship Evolution**: Adjusts character behavior dynamically based on a multi-dimensional relationship matrix with the user.
*   **Consistency Anchor**: Ensures the character's "Soul" remains intact, even during complex multi-character (Harem-Mode) scenarios.

### 3. The Narrator (Prose Synthesis & Stylistic Filtering)
The Narrator acts as the "Director of Photography."
*   **Atmospheric Prose**: Synthesizes character actions and dialogue into high-quality, descriptive prose.
*   **Multi-Format Rendering**: Dynamically switches between Visual Novel styles, third-person cinematic narratives, or casual chat based on user preference.
*   **Asset Orchestration**: Triggers visual changes (sprites, backdrops, weather overlays) in real-time to match the narrative mood.

### 4. The Continuity Guard (Closed-Loop Quality Assurance)
Our fail-safe mechanism:
*   **OOC (Out-of-Character) Detection**: Scans outputs for personality drift or generic "AI-isms."
*   **Hallucination Filter**: Blocks lore-breaking statements or formatting errors.
*   **Auto-Recalibration**: If a pass fails validation, the system automatically loops back and regenerates the logic until the quality threshold is met.

## 🌟 Ecosystem Capabilities

### 📚 Hyper-Persistent World Building
Project Neigo doesn't just remember facts; it remembers *relationships*. Our graph-based memory system allows characters to form opinions, hold grudges, and develop unique rapport over months of interaction.

### 🎭 Visual Novel 2.0 Integration
We have integrated a full-scale media engine directly into the AI workflow:
*   **Dynamic Sprites**: Characters change expressions and poses in synchronization with their emotional state.
*   **Atmospheric Layers**: Real-time manipulation of weather, time-of-day, and ambient effects.
*   **Rich Content Protocol**: Seamlessly blending text, audio-visual cues, and interactive UI elements.

### 📈 Scalable Foundation
*   **Monorepo Excellence**: Built with a modern `Turborepo` + `pnpm` stack for high-velocity development and deployment.
*   **High-Performance Backend**: Utilizing `Hono` on the `Bun` runtime for sub-millisecond API responses.
*   **Drizzle ORM & Postgres**: A robust, type-safe data layer designed for complex relational queries and massive vector datasets.
